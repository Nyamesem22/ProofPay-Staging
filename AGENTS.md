# Prototype Instructions

Run the local server yourself and open the preview in the browser available to this environment. Do not give the user server-start instructions when you can run it.

Before making substantial visual changes, use the Product Design plugin's `get-context` skill when the visual source is unclear or no longer matches the current goal. When the user gives durable prototype-specific design feedback, preferences, or decisions, record them in `AGENTS.md`.

For payment-channel coverage on the public landing page, use authentic provider brand assets rather than text-only placeholders. The current pitch vision includes Ghana (MTN MoMo, Telecel Cash, AT Money), Togo (Mixx by Yas, Moov Africa/Flooz), Nigeria (MoMo PSB, Smartcash PSB), plus bank-account and Visa-card categories. Use the full MoMo wordmark treatment for Ghana and an official Visa mark for cards. Keep a visible disclaimer that network availability depends on licensed partners and commercial agreements.

When implementing from a selected generated mock, treat that image as the source of truth for layout, component anatomy, density, spacing, color, typography, visible content, and hierarchy.

Build app UI in `src/`. Keep `.openai/hosting.json`, `worker/index.js`, `scripts/prepare-sites-build.mjs`, and `tests/sites-worker.test.mjs` intact so the same local prototype can be handed to Sites. Before a Sites handoff, run `npm run build` and `npm run test:sites`; the build must leave `dist/client/index.html`, `dist/server/index.js`, and `dist/.openai/hosting.json`.

The staff operations prototype is available at `/admin`. Treat it as a separate, role-based company workspace rather than an extension of the customer dashboard. Its durable information architecture includes payment operations, release control, transaction and account tracking, KYC/KYB verification, disputes, fraud and risk, safeguarded-funds reconciliation, partner health, immutable audit records, departments, staff task dashboards, and daily operations reporting.

The operating model assumes a mature regulated-fintech organisation with Executive, Payment Operations, Compliance and Legal, Risk and Fraud, Finance and Treasury, Technology, Customer Experience, Product and Design, Growth and Partnerships, People and Administration, and Internal Audit. Technology retains its specialist sub-departments: Product Engineering, DevOps and SRE, Cybersecurity, Data and Analytics, Quality Assurance, and IT Support.

In the release workflow, a transaction with every required check passed is automatically released and must not wait for human approval. Amber and red states show the exact failed control, assigned owner, next action and SLA. Payment retries reuse an idempotency key, check prior payout status and reconcile the final partner webhook to prevent duplicate releases.

The approved customer navigation is mobile-app style at every viewport: no vertical customer sidebar. Home, Activity, Messages and Settings stay in a fixed bottom bar, while Pay, USSD and Help remain available from the Home content and app header. The Home action label is “Pay”, not “New payment”. Signing out returns to the public ProofPay landing page and resets the customer view to Home.

Keep the customer portal progressively responsive and installable as a PWA. Phone layouts remain the source of truth, tablets widen the same app-style workspace, and laptop/desktop layouts use the available canvas without introducing a customer sidebar or stretching controls into oversized tiles. Preserve safe-area padding and the fixed app header/bottom navigation in installed standalone mode.

Every customer destination must share the same responsive shell width and dynamic viewport-height rules. Use the available foldable, tablet, and laptop canvas confidently, but keep intentional empty space on sparse pages instead of inventing content or inflating cards solely to fill it.

The Create payment Parties step must minimise typing. Reuse the signed-in customer’s verified profile and saved payment method, let them choose buyer/sender or seller/receiver, collect only the opposite party, support Individual and Business identities, detect supported mobile-money providers from country and number with a manual fallback, offer bank receiving details, and automatically open Agreement after verification or invitation succeeds.

The Create payment Agreement step is titled “Payment agreement”. It collects item or service, amount, agreement type, a typed agreement statement, and native date and time controls. Automatic agreement confirmation is internal: once buyer confirmation and delivery evidence are present, ProofPay checks them against the stored agreement statement before releasing funds. Do not show a customer-facing automation control on this step.

The Create payment Release rule step has the three selectable release-condition cards, with no safety panels, timeline or sidebar summary. Directly below the cards, it collects a short buyer confirmation statement, a received-item image and an explicit release checkbox; then ProofPay verifies these against the agreement before paying the seller's saved wallet or bank destination.

After a payment is released, its final customer screen is a transaction summary with the item image, buyer details, seller details, seller star rating and optional comment. Submitting the review closes the transaction while leaving the receipt available in Activity.

The wallet approval surface is full-screen within the customer app, without an inset dialog frame. Keep only the payment provider header, amount and breakdown, Cancel, and a short “Approve” action. Do not show the authorisation hero, PIN/check-phone notices, or pitch-simulation banner.

For the Create payment Parties step, keep the required controls and sequential verification logic, but do not show the modal title/subtitle, section headings for the counterparty, funding method or invitation, the provider-detection status copy, the invitation-channel label, or the Party Readiness side panel.

On phone-width Create payment Parties screens, omit the role-section heading and keep Business name/Contact person, Country/Mobile-money provider, Mobile wallet/Visa card, and Send an invite/Already registered in side-by-side pairs. Keep the mobile number on its own full-width row.

On that same screen, keep the buyer/seller role cards side-by-side, label account type "You are an", and use the short "Receiving wallet" label above the payout-destination choices.

Keep Home visually open and uncluttered. Do not add a secondary Pay/Track/Activity strip beneath the protected-balance card or a For you/Pay/Activity/Help tab row above the main service tiles. The service-promotion carousel is the final Home section; do not place current-payment, recent-activity, PIN-reminder or other content beneath it, and do not show a title, description, or slide counter above the campaign banner. The top app bar uses the transparent `proofpay-app-mark-transparent.png` P logo on the left, with notification, Help and Profile controls together on the right. Keep this entire top app bar fixed at the top of the customer shell while page content scrolls beneath it. Freeze each page's primary context directly below the app bar: the protected-balance card on Home, the Pay action/money summary/search toolbar on Activity, and the account card on Settings. Do not show an Activity page title or payment-count subtitle. Do not show a Settings page title, subtitle, or separate Protected badge above the account card. Settings has no default category selection and no category content beneath the four cards; each category opens its content in a closeable modal. The Activity status dropdown is the single status filter; do not add duplicate status tabs. Only the content below those frozen areas should scroll.

Activity history is ordered newest first and shows exactly five records per page. Keep Previous/Next pagination below the list, preserve search and status filtering across the complete history, and reset filtered results to page one. Activity rows use clear status icons without trailing arrows; clicking anywhere on a row opens the closable payment-record modal with complete transaction, party, amount, fee, channel, date, status and receipt details.

The notification bell opens a dedicated customer notification centre, not Settings. Show an unread badge and short, source-labelled notifications from the other transaction party, ProofPay Support, transaction status events and company/service announcements. Opening a notification marks it read and reveals its full message, date, source, relevant transaction context and a safe next action. Transaction-stage changes must create new unread notifications.

All customer pages use an MTN MoMo-inspired interaction model adapted to ProofPay branding: a prominent account or transaction card, icon-first action tiles, short tab labels, compact rows, large touch targets and progressive disclosure. Keep explanatory copy to one short line where possible, reveal inputs only when an action needs them, and never copy MTN branding or service names beyond real payment-provider references.

Home includes an automatically advancing ProofPay service-promotion carousel after the primary service grid. Its banners cover protected Pay, Track, USSD, Report/dispute protection, and Support/verified-agent help, rotate every few seconds, retain manual dot controls and end the Home content. Use the supplied ProofPay P artwork through the transparent `proofpay-app-mark-transparent.png` asset in the customer app header so the teal header shows through; do not add a white logo container or substitute a different mark.

Customer Messages uses a mobile inbox-first flow: show the conversation cards immediately without a page title, privacy banner, section heading or row arrows, then open one full-width chat with a clear Back control and persistent composer. Do not show separate privacy or PIN-warning banners inside the chat. The composer supports text, images, video, audio, PDF and common document files up to 20 MB, plus live microphone voice recording. Seller, verified-agent and ProofPay Support threads remain separate. ProofPay staff can see only messages explicitly sent to ProofPay Support; transaction-party chats are not exposed in the admin inbox. Customer support messages and attachments must appear in the staff Customer messages queue, and staff replies must return to the same customer thread and create a customer notification. The Agents page must repeat the rule that agents can guide customers but cannot ask for a MoMo PIN or personally approve payment, release or refund. Settings covers Profile, Security, Payment methods, Notifications and Accessibility. Help & support includes FAQs, chat, phone, USSD, agent routing and a support-request form.

The Home page exposes “Use *719#” as a quick action. The pitch demo must behave like a real feature phone: begin on the handset dialler, require the user to press `*719#` and the green call key, run the complete numbered USSD menu flow, accept a transaction confirmation code, support release and dispute outcomes, show session timing, and allow the red end key to close the session. It must always state that the ProofPay confirmation code is not the mobile-money PIN.
